Privacy Policy for Fast

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Fast ("the App") is a simple fasting timer designed to work primarily on your device.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

Fast does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or address.

Fasting records, settings, goals, reminders, and other App data are stored locally on your device.

Fast does not operate its own server for collecting or storing your fasting history.

2. Fasting and Health-Related Data

The App may store information such as:

  • Meal times
  • Fasting start and end times
  • Fasting duration
  • Fasting goals
  • Reminder settings
  • Fasting history and statistics

This information is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Fast is intended for general wellness and informational purposes only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional medical advice.

3. Advertising

The free version of Fast may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google AdMob may collect or process certain information, such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, approximate location, and information about interactions with advertisements.

Fast may also offer optional rewarded advertisements that allow users to temporarily access certain Pro features.

The handling of information by Google is governed by Google's own privacy policies.

You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its services at:
https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. In-App Purchases

Fast may offer a one-time Pro purchase through Google Play.

Payment and purchase information is processed by Google Play. Fast does not receive or store your full payment card or banking information.

The App may receive information necessary to confirm whether a Pro purchase has been successfully completed.

5. Notifications

Fast may request permission to send notifications for fasting goals and reminders.

Notification settings and fasting schedules are processed on your device.

You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Fast does not share your fasting history with third parties.

Third-party services used by the App, such as Google AdMob and Google Play, may process limited information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Fasting records and settings are stored locally on your device.

You can remove locally stored App data by using available deletion features within the App or by uninstalling the App.

Uninstalling the App may permanently delete locally stored data unless it has been backed up separately by your device or operating system.

8. Children's Privacy

Fast is not specifically directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through our own servers.

Third-party services used by the App may apply their own age-related policies and protections.

9. Security

Because Fast stores its primary user data locally on your device, the security of that data also depends on the security of your device and operating system.

We take reasonable steps to minimize unnecessary collection and transmission of user information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's features, third-party services, or legal requirements change.

Any updated version will be published with a revised "Last updated" date.
